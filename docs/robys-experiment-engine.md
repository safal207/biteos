# Robys Experiment Studio v0.1
Workspace #retail-robys-experiment. Fixed horizon draft and local aggregate review, pages uses synthetic scenarios. First hypothesis: adaptive layout vs unchanged menu; identical catalog, pricing, checkout and consent flow. Primary metric = assigned consenting eligible sessions with at least one successful cart mutation / all assigned consenting eligible sessions, including zero interaction. Not sales. 50/50 random first eligible session, immutable assignment per session across refresh, no demographics, no identity joining. Actual randomization, exposure recording and independent verification are NOT implemented. Require A/A check, exact source revisions/variant manifest, assignments including nonexposed, duplicate and missing-event audit, no overlapping experiment, agreed withdrawal handling and bot/multi-tab policy before production. Repeat visits may correlate, so session-level independence assumption must be reviewed. No causal or commercial claim.
Plan fields in robysExperiment.ts: exact schema, tenant, ID, catalog revision, 7–28 day fixed date window (end exclusive Istanbul), baseline and absolute MDE integer pp, error guard pp, fixed allocation/unit/metric. Sample planning normal approximation two-sided .05 / 80% power, min 100 per arm; requires prior actual baseline and feasibility review. Planning fields independent of demo/result. Result imports exact schema with embedded plan, operator-declared aggregate, per-arm assigned/cart/error counts, complete declaration. Not signed preregistration or verified provenance. No positive/negative result before close; primary 95% Newcombe-Wilson difference interval only if both planned samples reached and each arm has at least 10 successes/failures. SRM chi-square threshold 10.8276 (approx p=.001) separate gate; absolute B–A error difference over predeclared limit flags safety review even before close, not a statistically calibrated harm test. No auto-stop, launch, winner, billing or change deployment. Positive review means interval above zero, not proof of economically meaningful lift; require independent evidence, practical significance and owner review. No paid SDK. Full business contribution tracked separately via commercial pilot, not assigned to web variants until approved reconciled linkage exists. No real data in repo.
