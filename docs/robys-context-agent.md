# Robys Context Assistant v0.1

Manual scenario engine and TR/RU/EN workspace #retail-robys-context. Context is date, local hour, manually chosen weather, explicit visit intent and optional favorite or item budget. Real catalog IDs/prices, deterministic reason codes, unavailable-item filter at model boundary. No live weather, GPS, tracking, client segmentation, notification, discount, auto-order or public menu mutation. Stock and catalog hours (09:00–00:00) are unverified. Severe weather scenario suppresses offers. Missing weather is neutral. No stock adapter; UI assumes catalog candidates pending availability. Not actual demand prediction.

Calendar source: Turkish Ministry of Education (13 June 2026) https://www.meb.gov.tr/2026-2027-egitim-ogretim-yili-takvimi-aciklandi/haber/41057/tr . Reviewed school-break ranges 16–20 Nov 2026, 25 Jan–5 Feb 2027, 8–12 Mar 2027. Fixed-date occasions are selected, not exhaustive; no inferred future academic dates. Local events/campus calendar need explicit owner review. Public local context: Ahmet Tuncer primary school at Uğur Mumcu Cd 164 https://ahmettuncerio.meb.k12.tr/meb_iys_dosyalar/07/06/702132/okulumuz_hakkinda.html ; public library Uğur Mumcu Cd 10 https://okuldisiogrenme.eba.gov.tr/mekan-detay/gazipasa-ilce-halk-kutuphanesi-5692 ; ALKÜ vocational school Pazarcı 10007 Sok No 1 https://gazipasamyo.alanya.edu.tr/ . These are context, not proof of foot traffic or individual demographics. Family/study/social use only self-selected intent. No targeting children or tracking visits to schools.

Weather provider decision: MET Norway Locationforecast https://api.met.no/ and https://docs.api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService.html allows reasonable commercial use with attribution, identifying UA and caching. Human authority must review/accept provider terms before network use. Future adapter server-side fixed cafe coordinates (not visitor address), cached response/Expires/If-Modified-Since, source/time/freshness; unavailable/stale weather => neutral, never fabricate. Official warnings distinct from ordinary forecast, prevent unsafe marketing. Open-Meteo hosted free endpoint restricted to non-commercial use. No new paid dependency or new request in this change.

Future: owner-approved suggestion cards instead of changed full menu; quiet hours, separate marketing consent, revocation, frequency cap, valid availability, no age inference/precise geolocation. Keep regular menu accessible. Compare approved suggestions versus baseline for observed behavior, independently reconcile sales. Do not infer causality from weather alone.

## International calendar extension
Source range 2026-09-14 through 2027-12-31. Fixed annual civil and cultural occasions, second Sunday of May / third Sunday of June, reviewed 2027 Turkish religious feast dates (not generalized), simultaneous occasions, 30-day preview across year boundary. Dates are Istanbul local; no assumption every guest celebrates. International Coffee Day: https://ico.org/international-coffee-day/ ; local cultural relevance example Halloween 2026 Antalya/Alanya: https://www.biletimgo.com/ozel-gun/halloween/antalya ; Turkish 2027 official calendar https://blog.turkishairlines.com/en/2027-public-holidays-in-turkiye/ ; local occasions https://www.ciceksiparisiantalya.com/TR/ozel-gunler . Optional Halloween/Christmas merchandising is off unless explicitly selected in scenario; no inference of nationality or religion. Memorial day Nov 10 suppresses promotion; national/religious holidays otherwise neutral unless specifically appropriate. No stock, opening-hours or real campaign verification. Existing products only, no invented seasonal recipes. Industry/theme days are marketing planning labels, not public holidays. Nothing is published or sent.
