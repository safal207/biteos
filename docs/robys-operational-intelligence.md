# Robys Operational Intelligence v0.1
Workspace #retail-robys-operations. Local aggregate ingredient plan, no network/storage/stock mutation; Pages uses synthetic recipes and lots only. Reuses the actual forecast function for the demo latte weekday baseline, not a copied forecast value. Flat white volume, recipes and lots are illustrative, not a café recipe or verified availability. Existing Go recipe-cost and lifetime modules retained unchanged. Real local input is a same-day operator-declared snapshot with exact schema in robysOperations.ts: pinned public catalog, selected product IDs, planned units, ingredient units g/ml/piece, per-portion recipe quantities, reserve, pack quantities, lots with quantity, declared release and use-by. Manual plan may be informed by forecast but is not a verified forecast import. Sum ingredient quantities across selected SKUs; no duplicate recipes/lot/ingredient identifiers. Eligible lots require operator release and use-by strictly after planning date; FEFO allocation for review; unknown, held, same-day and expired stock excluded conservatively. Dates and releases do not prove food safety; require physical inspection, cold-chain/opened-package rules and recipe/stock validation. Near-date remainder after illustrative allocation is a review signal, not predicted waste. Packaging count rounded up to cover selected-plan gap plus reserve, not an order. Partial menu coverage: other sales, reservations, wastage, recipe yield, variants, deliveries and shared commitments not represented; no whole-cafe purchase recommendation. No cost, profit, supplier price or food safety claim. Local file data not uploaded, no owner authentication. TR/RU/EN. No new dependencies. Draft only; CI required.
