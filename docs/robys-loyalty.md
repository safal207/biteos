# Robys Loyalty Intelligence v0.1

Local file projection in #retail-robys-loyalty; public Pages synthetic only. Accepts an owner-authorized single snapshot of currently consenting loyalty members and settled, operator-reconciled POS orders, with two adjacent complete 14-day windows in Istanbul (end exclusive). No payment proof, no data collection, no account enrollment, personal recommendations, identity join with site traffic, or message dispatch. Do not export raw files to GitHub. File remains local; only derived counts are retained in component state.

Input schema robys.loyalty-input.v1: tenantId robys, currency TRY, source operator-reconciled-pos, completeForConsentingMembers true (operator declaration, not proof), startDay/endExclusive, members [{memberKey: random 32 hex, consentFrom: UTC ISO timestamp, activeConsent:true}], orders [{orderKey: independently assigned opaque 32 hex, memberKey, settledAt: UTC ISO timestamp, netMinor: positive reconciled TRY minor units after discounts, lines:[{productId,quantity,netMinor}]}]. No names, phone, email or web session IDs. One line per product per order. Only fully settled non-refunded orders; partial/full refunds must be excluded consistently by operator; no inferred adjustments. Only opted-in orders after consent. Snapshot contains exactly the prior and current windows; omit withdrawn members and all their records. Completeness and source require separate review. No true new-customer claim: first seen means absent from prior 14-day window. Repeat purchaser = at least two distinct settled orders in current window; favorite rankings use 2+ orders and 3+ distinct local days over 14 days, not exact visits or weekly frequency. Retention = prior active members with current order / prior active members. Mean check = included net / included orders, not all cafe revenue.

Small group guard: both active and prior counts at least five; product rows require five buyers. Not a formal anonymization guarantee (derived subgroups and time bins may be small); keep all reports owner-only. Consent and deletion policy, secure POS export, handling refunds and actual enrollment must be agreed before live deployment. No mandatory paid dependencies. Next: week-based 3+ purchase frequency, cohort privacy review, account/loyalty integration only under approved terms.
