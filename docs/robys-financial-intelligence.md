# Robys Financial Intelligence v0.1
Workspace #retail-robys-financial. Uses the existing Operations snapshot exact recipes and pinned actual public catalog SKU/price; demo uses same illustrative ingredient pack costs as existing Go preset, with explicit 100% yield and 5 TRY other variable expense, 2% gross payment fee, 0% illustrative tax (not a real rate). Local bundle {plan,costs}, both strict schemas; costs must match day, catalog, tenant, recipe ingredient IDs/units and selected SKU coverage exactly. Missing cost rejects rather than imputes zero. Net sales = floor(gross/(1+declared tax rate)); payment fee ceiling on gross; ingredient cost uses exact integer rational ceil with declared usable yield; other variable per portion declared, excluding tax/fee/ingredients to avoid double count. Assumes comparable single SKU portion and unchanged unit costs at volume. Margin = contribution/net sales; review threshold 20%, not business policy. Discounts 0–90% in whole points, max discount at floor computed within this range; no price activation. Matching portions = ceil(previous planned contribution / discounted per-serving contribution), excludes shared campaign expense and capacity, may be infeasible. Portfolio scenario holds volumes fixed and subtracts declared incremental campaign cost once; no demand response, profit, tax validation, supplier quotes, inventory feasibility or realized revenue. Fixed rent/labor not implied; include only variable components in other expense. Local imports only; Pages synthetic. No network, storage, owner auth or external effects. Reuse Go recipe-cost logic conceptually (conservative rounding), do not change Go API or production café. CI pending.
